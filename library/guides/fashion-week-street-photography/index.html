Fashion week street photography

  • street-photography
  • fashion-week
  • crowd-safety

Fashion week street photography is a reusable method, not a promise that a particular Trip has selected a show.

  1. Recheck the official calendar close to the date; show addresses may remain private or change after a provisional schedule is published.
  2. If a public exterior location is confirmed and selected, arrive before the published show time rather than trying to enter an invitation-only event.
  3. Use a 70–200mm-equivalent lens to work behind the densest press line. Prefer the relationship between people and city architecture over competing for the front row.
  4. Keep exits, security lines and pedestrian flow clear. Do not follow subjects into private access areas or block vehicles.
  5. Create a dated ShootSession only after the Trip has selected a location and time. Until then, retain the candidate as option/trigger/rationale and rely on the volatile ResearchFact.
